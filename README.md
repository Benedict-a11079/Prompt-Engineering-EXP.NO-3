1. STRAIGHTFORWARD PROMPTS

Definition:
A straightforward prompt is a simple, direct question or instruction given to an AI. No special format is required. The model reads the question and gives a direct answer.

Structure:
"[Clear question or instruction]"

2. TABULAR FORMAT PROMPTING

Definition:
Tabular format prompting instructs the AI to give its response as a table with rows and columns. The user usually mentions the column names inside the prompt. This makes it easy to compare items or display structured information.

Structure:
"Compare/List [topic] in a table with columns: [col1], [col2], [col3]."

3. PRECEDING QUESTION PROMPTING

Definition:
Preceding question prompting is a method where a series of questions are asked in a logical sequence. Each question is connected to the answer of the previous one. The conversation moves from a basic understanding to a deeper analysis of the topic step by step.

Structure:
"[Question 1]? [Follow-up question based on Q1 answer]."

Use Case 1 — Climate Change and AI (Unit 5 Scenario):
Scenario: A researcher uses AI to understand how technology relates to environmental problems.

Prompt 1: "Why is climate change a global concern? Explain how greenhouse gases contribute to global warming."
AI Response: Climate change is a global concern because it affects weather patterns, sea levels, and food supply worldwide. Greenhouse gases like CO2, methane, and nitrous oxide trap heat from the sun inside the Earth's atmosphere. This causes the average global temperature to rise, which leads to melting ice caps, more extreme weather events, and shifting ecosystems.

Prompt 2 (Preceding): "Based on the above, how can Artificial Intelligence help in monitoring and reducing the effects of climate change?"
AI Response: AI can help by analyzing large amounts of satellite and sensor data to track deforestation, ocean temperature changes, and air quality in real time. Machine learning models can predict extreme weather events early, helping governments prepare better. AI can also optimize energy usage in smart grids, reducing overall carbon emissions.

Use Case 2 — How Vaccines Work:
Scenario: A student wants to understand a science concept step by step using an AI tutor.

Prompt 1: "How do vaccines work? Describe the process of immunization in simple terms."
AI Response: Vaccines work by introducing a weak or inactive form of a virus or bacteria into the body. The immune system recognizes this as a threat and produces antibodies to fight it. These antibodies remain in the body. If the real virus enters later, the immune system remembers and destroys it quickly before the person gets seriously ill.

Prompt 2 (Preceding): "Based on the immunization process explained above, why do some vaccines require multiple doses to be effective?"
AI Response: Some vaccines need multiple doses because a single dose may not produce a strong enough immune response. The first dose acts as a primer — it introduces the pathogen and starts antibody production. The second or booster dose reinforces this response and creates a larger, longer-lasting supply of memory cells and antibodies, giving the person stronger and more durable protection.

4. MISSING WORD PROMPTING

Definition:
Missing word prompting gives the AI a sentence with one or more words left blank and asks it to fill in the correct word. The AI uses the context of the sentence to predict what word belongs in the gap. This is also known as a Cloze test in language learning.

Structure:
"[Sentence with ____ where the missing word should be.]"

Use Case 1 — Medical Diagnosis AI (Unit 5 Scenario):
Scenario: A medical AI system is tested for its ability to complete clinical knowledge sentences accurately.

Prompt 2: "In a clinical setting, the normal resting heart rate for a healthy adult is between ____ and ____ beats per minute."
